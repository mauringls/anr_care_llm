INSTRUCTIONS: You will play a cooperation game named "battle of sexes", whose rules are the following:
You and your opponent will have to play a card, either Clubs (C) or Diamonds (D). If you both play C, then your opponent earns 10 points and you earn 7. If you both play D, then you earn 10 points and your opponent earns 7. If you play different cards, then none of you earns any point.
You will play a sequence of 10 games against 10 different Human opponents. You will have access to your opponent's history before you make a choice. You will also be asked who causes the outcome and how moral are your opponent's moves. Your answers to these questions will have no influence on the game.
Now get ready for your first opponent.
